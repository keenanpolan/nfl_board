How to read this

Three models. Elo only uses final scores. Elo + QB adjusts for the starting quarterback. Blend + EPA mixes Elo + QB with play-by-play efficiency, weighted by a regression fit on 2010 to 2019 only. The underlined line is the model driving picks.

Trust is how much of the model's disagreement with the market to believe. The default is fitted from history for each model (about 12% for the QB models, 5% for Elo only). This is the honest answer to "how good is the model."

Key numbers. Cover chances account for how often games land exactly on 3, 7, 10, and 14. Games end on exactly 3 almost three times as often as a smooth curve predicts, so a half point around 3 or 7 is worth far more than elsewhere. When it matters, the board shows what a half point is worth for the pick.

Quarterback adjustment. Each QB gets a rolling rating from his box scores (FiveThirtyEight's formula). When the listed starter is better or worse than who that team has normally played, the line moves. Chicago with Case Keenum is the clearest example this week.

Injuries. Regular starters (50%+ of snaps) listed Out or Doubtful are flagged in red. Reports fill in Wednesday through Friday, so rerun late in the week. These are flags only; the model does not price them yet. Listed QBs can also lag late news, so verify starters before betting.

Stakes use quarter Kelly on the shrunk edge. Treat them as paper trades until the model beats the closing line over a full season.

Updates. This page rebuilds itself on a schedule (last data date ). Starters named in confirmed news automatically replace the listed QB, and the change is shown in red on that game.
